3. **CJK/multilingual contamination** in Cursor-era drafts (verified lines): `agents/types.ts:70`, `ai/local-rules-provider.ts:26,44,84`, `components/layout/Shell.tsx:627`, `modules/agents/AgentsScreen.tsx:431,439`, `modules/dashboard/DashboardScreen.tsx:52`, `modules/service/ServiceScreen.tsx:26,29` — including corrupted _classification regexes_, not just labels. Blanket REJECT on these files.
